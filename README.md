# HYPERSHOT 3D - Alpine Wilderness Open World FPS

A high-performance, browser-based 3D multiplayer first-person shooter set in a photorealistic, lush **Alpine River Valley Open World** (inspired by Three.js natural wilderness environments and classic arcade FPS movement).

Built as a standalone web application using **React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Rapier 3D Physics, Node.js, and Colyseus WebSockets**.

---

## 🌲 Alpine River Valley Open World

- **Lush Valley & Procedural Rolling Hills**:
  - Continuous alpine meadows with vertex-colored grass gradients, forested mountain ridges, and shaded hillsides.
- **Winding Alpine River**:
  - Realistic river canyon with rocky embankments, gravel shores, and sparkling water surface.
- **Dense Procedural Forest & Foliage**:
  - Hundreds of instanced, multi-tiered evergreen spruce pine trees and mountain birch trees.
  - Thousands of fluttering wild grass tufts and fern patches.
  - Natural river boulders, granite rocks, and fallen tactical logs for combat cover.
- **Tactical Open-World Structures**:
  - Rustic wooden river crossing bridge across the central canyon.
  - West and East ridge mountain outpost cabins for vantage perches.
- **Atmospheric Horizon & Lighting**:
  - Encircling 3D snow-capped alpine mountain peaks on the horizon.
  - Golden natural sunlight with dynamic shadows, blue skybox, and misty alpine depth fog.

## 👤 4 Operative Character Classes with 3D Preview

Players select their combat operative in the lobby with a live rotating 3D preview:

1. **Vanguard (Tactical Assault)**:
   - Balanced frontline combatant with cobalt-blue tactical visor optics, reinforced shoulder armor, and versatile stats.
2. **Phantom (Cyber Scout)**:
   - Lightweight stealth operative with aerodynamic carbon weave, ultra-agile strafing agility, and emerald-green optics.
3. **Juggernaut (Heavy Enforcer)**:
   - Imposing titanium exoskeleton with heavy blast helmet, reinforced blast plating, and glowing crimson forcefield shielding.
4. **Spectre (Cyber Infiltrator)**:
   - Futuristic cybernetic infiltrator with precision targeting sensors and amber holographic energy nodes.

## 📱 Mobile Phone & 💻 Desktop Controls

- **Mobile Landscape Touch**:
  - Virtual left analog joystick with auto-sprint threshold.
  - Right thumb swipe-to-aim touch zone with inertia smoothing.
  - Dedicated glowing FIRE, JUMP, and SPRINT action buttons.
- **Desktop / Laptop**:
  - `WASD`: Move
  - Mouse: Look & Aim (click canvas for pointer lock)
  - Left Mouse Click / Hold: Rapid Auto-Fire
  - `SPACE`: Jump
  - `SHIFT`: Sprint
  - `TAB`: Match Leaderboard
